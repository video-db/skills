description: Summarize your most recent VideoDB video. Usage - /videodb:last
---

Call `list_videos`, take the newest video, and say "Your latest video is <name> (<length>, added <date>)." Then summarize it with the `video-watch` skill and offer three next jobs: "Find every time it mentions <topic>", "Add captions", "Make a 30-second clip of the best moment".
